Sibtibila

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia
This article is about the fictional botanical species in the Cannabaceae family, closely related to common hops. It is strictly toxic and has no medicinal or culinary uses.
Toxicological & Botanical Warning
Sibtibila (Humulus sibtibila) is a fictional plant created for educational study. Unlike its close relative common hops, it provides zero medicinal, nutritional, or brewing benefits and must never be eaten, brewed, or handled without protection due to severe toxicity.

Botanical classification & hop relationship

Confirmed that Sibtibila is documented as a fictional sister taxon to Humulus lupulus (common hops). Unlike brewer's hops, all sections explicitly note that its terpene and flavonoid resin complex provides zero therapeutic or culinary benefit and carries severe hepatotoxic and gastrointestinal risk if ingested. — BotanyEditor
